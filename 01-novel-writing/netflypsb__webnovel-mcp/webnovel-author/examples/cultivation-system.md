# Example: Cultivation System Design

## System Name: Nine Heavens Ascension Path

### Core Concept
Cultivators absorb Qi from heaven and earth to strengthen body and soul, advancing through stages toward immortality.

### Cultivation Stages

| Stage | Substages | Average Time | Power Description | Social Status |
|-------|-----------|--------------|-------------------|---------------|
| **Mortal** | - | N/A | No cultivation | Common |
| **Qi Condensation** | Early/Mid/Late/Peak | 5-10 years | Superhuman strength, extended lifespan +50 years | Outer Disciple |
| **Foundation Establishment** | Early/Mid/Late/Peak | 10-20 years | Flight, elemental manipulation, +200 years | Inner Disciple |
| **Golden Core** | Early/Mid/Late/Peak | 20-50 years | Soul attacks, domain creation, +500 years | Core Disciple/Elder |
| **Nascent Soul** | Early/Mid/Late/Peak | 50-100 years | Divine sense, soul separation, +1000 years | Sect Master level |
| **Void Transformation** | Early/Mid/Late/Peak | 100-200 years | Space manipulation, +2000 years | Regional powerhouse |
| **Immortal Ascension** | Varies | Rare | True immortality, world creation | Heaven-defying |

### Breakthrough Requirements
- **Qi Condensation → Foundation**: First tribulation (3 lightning strikes)
- **Foundation → Golden Core**: Compress all Qi into golden core
- **Golden Core → Nascent Soul**: Birth soul from core (50% failure rate)
- **Nascent Soul → Void**: Nine tribulations (90% mortality)
- **Void → Immortal**: Heavenly Dao acknowledgment (0.01% success)

### Resources & Aids
- **Spirit Stones**: Currency and cultivation aid
- **Pills**: Breakthrough assistance, healing, temporary power boost
- **Techniques**: Rare methods improve cultivation speed
- **Treasures**: Weapons, armor, artifacts that channel Qi

### Combat Power Scale
- Same stage: Victory depends on techniques, talent, and experience
- One stage higher: Overwhelming advantage (10x power)
- Two stages higher: Instant victory (100x power)
- Exception: Heaven-defying talent can bridge one small realm gap

### Tracking in Story
Character sheet always shows:
- Current stage and substage
- Time at current stage
- Breakthrough proximity
- Limiting factors (resources, comprehension, tribulation readiness)

Change log tracks every advancement with:
- Previous stage
- New stage
- Resources consumed
- Tribulation survived (if any)
- Time taken
- Chapter number

This prevents:
- ❌ Sudden unexplained power jumps
- ❌ Inconsistent power scaling
- ❌ Forgotten cultivation requirements
- ❌ Timeline contradictions
