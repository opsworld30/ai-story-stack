# Character System

Use this when designing protagonists, antagonists, supporting characters, relationships, character arcs, dialogue voices, or character knowledge states.

For 人物立体化, 性格阶段, 心理变化, 情感变化, 人物弧光深化, or when a role feels like a plot function instead of a living person, also use `references/15-character-depth-arc.md`.

## Voice DNA

For each important character, define:

- Sentence length: clipped, flowing, formal, casual, evasive, aggressive.
- Vocabulary: refined, local, technical, childish, official, poetic, blunt.
- Emotional leak: silence, jokes, orders, questions, sarcasm, action, avoidance.
- Relationship marker: title, nickname, no name, honorific, insult, private phrase.
- Forbidden mode: what would make the character sound generic.

## Character Knowledge

Track what each character knows:

```markdown
| 角色 | 已知 | 未知 | 误解 | 读者知道但角色不知道 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
```

Use this before writing scenes with secrets, betrayal, investigation, romance misunderstanding, or political conflict.

## Relationship Design

Every important relationship needs a dynamic verb:

- protects
- envies
- manipulates
- doubts
- owes
- fears
- tests
- hides from
- competes with
- depends on

Avoid static labels such as "friend", "enemy", or "love interest" unless paired with behavior.

## Review Questions

- Does this character make decisions only they would make?
- Could another character say the same line without changing the scene? If yes, sharpen voice.
- What does this character refuse to admit?
- What stage is this character currently in, and what stage are they not allowed to jump to yet?
- What emotion is visible on the surface, and what emotion is hidden underneath?
- What exact trigger makes this character soften, attack, retreat, lie, or lose control?
- What does the reader understand that this character cannot yet see?
- What relationship pressure will create the next scene?
