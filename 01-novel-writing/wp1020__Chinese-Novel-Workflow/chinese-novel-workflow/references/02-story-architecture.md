# Story Architecture

Use this when designing worldbuilding, power systems, organizations, conflicts, secrets, or the long-range story engine.

## Architecture Layers

Build from hard constraints outward:

1. Canon rules: facts that cannot be violated.
2. Power rules: what characters can and cannot do.
3. Social rules: class, sect, family, company, court, academy, city, or nation logic.
4. Conflict engine: why peace is impossible.
5. Information engine: what is hidden, who knows it, and when readers learn it.
6. Cost engine: what every victory consumes.

## Rules

- Every rule must generate constraints, choices, or consequences.
- Avoid decorative worldbuilding that never changes character decisions.
- A power system needs limits, costs, and failure cases.
- A faction needs a goal, not just a name.
- A secret needs an owner, a victim, and a reveal window.
- Conflict should escalate by changing the protagonist's options, not only by raising enemy strength.

## Common Failures

- Setting dump before scene pressure exists.
- Power level inflation without emotional or tactical cost.
- Organizations with identical motives.
- Secrets that exist only for surprise and do not change the plot.
- Villains who wait passively for the protagonist.
