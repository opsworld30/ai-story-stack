# Character Depth And Arc

Use this when the user asks for 人物立体化, 性格阶段, 心理变化, 情感变化, 人物弧光深化, 角色跑偏修正, 人物不够立体, or when a character card only describes function, goal, fear, and voice but lacks an inner operating system.

## Core Rule

A character is not only a function in the plot. Define how the character protects themselves, what they misunderstand, how emotion leaks, and how each story stage changes their self-image, relationships, and choices.

Do not add soft redemption, romance, trauma, or hidden kindness just to make a character "complex". Depth means consistent inner logic, not washing away the role.

## When To Use

Use this after `references/03-character-system.md` when:

- The character feels like a tag: 法治执行者, 佛子, 真千金, 霸总, 白月光, 假千金, etc.
- The outline has plot changes but not psychological or emotional changes.
- The prose keeps making the character react generically.
- A villain risks sudden洗白 or a protagonist risks losing their core method.
- A romance, revenge, family, legal, or identity story needs stable emotional progression.

## Stage Design

For each major character, define 3-6 stages. A stage must include an external situation and an internal turn.

Good stage names are specific:

- 待宰自救期
- 证据控制期
- 法律进攻期
- 真相动摇期
- 清算完成后的空落期
- 重新选择自我期

Avoid vague stage names:

- 成长期
- 黑化期
- 变强期
- 甜蜜期

## Character Depth Patterns

### Revenge / Legal Counterattack Protagonist

Depth should come from method, body cost, restraint, and moral boundary.

- What makes the protagonist choose procedure instead of pure violence?
- Which part is tactical calm, and which part is emotional numbness?
- What physical or social weakness keeps each victory from becoming effortless?
- What line will the protagonist not cross even when angry?
- After victory, what remains unresolved emotionally?

### Romance / Chasing Spouse / Misunderstanding Pair

Depth should come from two incompatible protection mechanisms.

- One character may pursue certainty; the other may suppress expression.
- Define what each person thinks love should look like.
- Define what each person mistakes for indifference, control, dignity, or safety.
- Do not let confession erase prior harm; define what repair requires.

### Family Revenge / Identity Reveal Protagonist

Depth should come from belonging, grief, shame, and chosen family.

- What does the protagonist still want from family, even if they deny it?
- What object or memory carries the emotional center?
- What changes after the public win, when there is no audience left?
- What is the difference between revenge satisfaction and grief release?

### Antagonist

Depth should come from consistent desire, fear, and self-justification, not redemption.

- What lie does the antagonist tell themselves?
- What status, relationship, or secret are they protecting?
- What makes them escalate instead of stop?
- What specific evidence or scene finally breaks their mask?

## Output Rules

- Preserve the original role function and genre promise.
- Add inner logic before adding backstory.
- Make every stage writable: include actions, speech changes, and emotional leakage.
- Keep red lines concrete enough to prevent drift during drafting.
- If source material is thin, infer conservatively and mark assumptions.

## Quality Gate

Before using a character file for outline, scene cards, or prose generation, confirm:

- The character has a stable core and a changing surface.
- Each major stage changes psychology, emotion, action, or relationship.
- The character has soft spots without violating role red lines.
- The villain has logic without becoming accidentally sympathetic beyond the user's intent.
- The protagonist can win while still paying a physical, emotional, social, or moral cost.
- The voice DNA changes under pressure without becoming another character.
