# Narrative Readability Standard

Use this as the shared readability and web-fiction texture standard for any Chinese narrative unit: chapter, scene, short story, opening hook, serialized episode, plot-driven prose, or rewritten fragment.

## Core Principle

Make the text easy to enter, fast to follow, and concrete enough to feel. Preserve story truth, genre promise, and character voice while improving rhythm, scene presence, dialogue, and mobile readability.

Do not force every project into the same intensity. Legal comedy, romance, family drama, apocalypse, revenge, and slice-of-life need different texture density.

## Narrative Unit

A narrative unit can be:

- a chapter
- a scene
- a short story
- an opening hook
- a climactic fragment
- a continuation
- a rewritten passage
- a plot-driven oral-story style draft

Judge the unit by function, not length:

- What pressure opens it?
- What changes by the end?
- What does the protagonist choose or discover?
- What information, relationship, danger, or emotion moves forward?
- What hook, payoff, reversal, or aftertaste remains?

## Sentence Rules

Aim for readable, clear, image-first prose.

- Prefer one sentence for one action, sightline, or emotional turn.
- Split long sentences by action change, sightline change, emotion change, or information turn.
- Put the main action before explanation whenever possible.
- Separate main action from psychology, cause, and background.
- Replace abstract feeling with body reaction, object handling, silence, timing, or visible choice.
- Do not repeat the same meaning in different wording.
- Do not let one sentence carry action, setting, backstory, emotion, and judgment at once.

Useful patterns:

| Pattern | Use when | Direction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 动作先行 | opening, transition, sudden event | Person acts first, consequence follows. |
| 观察落点 | surprise, fear, attraction, suspicion | Character sees one concrete thing, then reacts. |
| 反应承接 | message, threat, reveal, interruption | External stimulus triggers immediate body/action response. |
| 重点单拎 | reversal, death notice, confession, reveal, hard decision | Put the key sentence or image in its own paragraph. |

## Anti-Translation-ese & Pure Chinese Syntax (中式纯正语感与去翻译腔)

To prevent machine flavor and maintain natural Chinese flow:

- **De-westernize sentence structure (去欧化句式)**:
  - Do not pile more than two modifiers/adjectives before a noun (e.g., replace `“他那双写满了疲惫和愤怒的眼睛”` with `“他的眼睛写满疲惫，还有愤怒”`).
  - Shorten modifier phrases: convert subordinate clauses into sequential active clauses.
- **Be-sentence protection gate (被字句误伤保护)**:
  - Do not treat every `被` sentence as AI residue. A normal 被字句 is valid when it expresses pressure, victimhood, restraint, humiliation, being forced/trapped, unknown or unimportant actors, POV-limited knowledge, character voice, or a smoother Chinese idiom.
  - Only revise unnecessary passive voice, westernized passive syntax, and passive sensory mediation. Before changing a 被字句, ask whether `被` carries scene pressure or character posture. If yes, keep it.
  - Good to keep: `她被按在椅子上，手腕硌着扶手。` / `他被困在电梯里。`
  - Good to revise: `门外的声音被她听见了。` -> `她听见门外有声音。`; `一种冰冷的感觉被他的脚底感知到。` -> `冰意从脚底钻上来。`
- **Omit logical conjunctions (省略逻辑关联词)**:
  - Delete unnecessary logical connectors like `“因为……所以……”`, `“虽然……但是……”`, `“不仅……而且……”` if the chronology or context implies the relationship. Chinese relies on implicit coherence (意合).
- **Prune redundant pronouns and copulas (修剪代词与系动词)**:
  - Remove unnecessary subjects like `“他”`, `“她”` when the actor is obvious from the paragraph flow.
  - Avoid nominalized helper verbs or abstract markers (e.g., replace `“进行了激烈的反抗”` with `“激烈反抗”`; replace `“做出了选择”` with `“做出选择”`).

## Dialogue Rules

Dialogue must do story work. Each important exchange should do at least one of:

- give information
- create conflict
- force a decision
- change a relationship
- push action forward
- expose a lie, weakness, desire, or pressure point

Make dialogue sound relational:

- Match age, identity, education, social position, intimacy, and power relation.
- Let familiar characters omit background they both know.
- Use interruption, denial, counter-question, evasion, topic shift, teasing, or silence.
- Put subtext under important emotion; do not make characters announce every feeling.
- Let enemies attack face, leverage, weakness, or choices, not only shout threats.
- Let friends sound less polite and more interactive.
- Let parents, mentors, lovers, and rivals express care or hostility through habit, command, sarcasm, restraint, or action.

## Mobile Reading Layout

- No manual first-line indentation.
- Use blank lines between paragraphs.
- Keep paragraphs sparse; avoid visual black blocks.
- Put dialogue on its own paragraph.
- Separate dialogue from action when tension is high.
- Put major reversals, key judgments, hard hooks, and high-impact physical reactions in standalone paragraphs.
- If a paragraph mixes environment, action, system/state, and emotion, split it by focus.

## Paragraph De-Formatting

- Avoid equal-weight paragraphing.
- Do not make every paragraph contain action + psychology + explanation.
- Use pressure to decide paragraph length.
- Allow one-line paragraphs for shock, recognition, refusal, or delayed emotion.
- Let some actions stand without explanation.
- Let some actions stand without explanation only when their function is clear from pressure, object, consequence, or the next choice.
- Let dialogue sometimes land without summary.
- Use `references/16-attention-driven-prose.md` when prose exposes outline structure or feels too evenly formatted.

## Trust-The-Reader Standard

Adapted from Humanizer-zh for fiction: the prose should trust the reader instead of explaining the scene like an article.

- Do not inflate ordinary actions into symbolic meaning: avoid "标志着", "象征着", "证明了", "彰显了", "见证了" unless the POV character would naturally use that register.
- Do not add slogan-like summaries after a scene has already landed.
- Do not write quote-ready maxims just to sound powerful. A hook line is allowed only when it belongs to the character, genre, or chapter ending.
- Do not use "不仅……而且……", "不是……而是……", or "从X到Y" to manufacture depth. Replace with action, contrast in behavior, or a concrete result.
- Do not over-qualify simple perceptions with "某种程度上", "可以说", "似乎有些", or "潜在地" unless uncertainty is the point of the scene.
- Do not create three-part adjective or noun lists for completeness. Fiction can be uneven; pressure should shape rhythm.
- Prefer specific scene facts over broad commentary. Instead of saying a choice is "关键", show what it costs, blocks, exposes, or changes.

## Rhetorical Intensity Protection

Web fiction may use exaggerated adjectives, heightened emotional phrasing, repetition, dramatic contrast, and strong sensory language.

Do not remove intensity just to make prose look restrained.

Keep exaggerated wording when it has at least one function:

- Character voice
- Genre promise
- Pressure or humiliation
- Suspense or dread
- Comedy or sarcasm
- 爽点 / payoff
- Body reaction
- Relationship tension
- Rhythm or hook

Revise it only when it is empty emphasis:

- no concrete action
- no object or sensory anchor
- no character-specific voice
- no pressure change
- no consequence
- replaceable by any other scene

Example:

Keep:
`疼得她眼前发黑，指甲在床单上抓出一道皱痕。`

Revise:
`她感受到了一种极其强烈而难以忍受的疼痛。`
## Common AI And Drafting Lesions

Scan for these and revise:

| Lesion | Fix direction |
| --- | --- |
| 然而 / 但是 / 可 as hard transition | Let action, sound, movement, or scene change interrupt. |
| 不是 A，而是 B | Replace with observation progression, action result, or character stance. The compound "然后……不是……而是……" is the strongest AI fingerprint: it flattens experience into a teacher-correction rhythm. Delete "不是A" when readers wouldn't misunderstand; convert "而是B" into POV sensory result. |
| 然后 as lazy temporal connector | Replace with sensory consequence, object response, body reaction, or causal consequence. If two events are merely sequential without causal or perceptual link, question whether both are needed. |
| 像是 / 仿佛 / 宛如 too dense | Keep only the strongest metaphor; use concrete result elsewhere. |
| 感官中介 / 被动感官中介: 脚底的触感、耳边传来、被他的脚底感知到 | Write direct contact, sound source, object friction, body response. This is not a ban on normal 被字句. |
| 上帝视角总结 | Restrict to what the character can see, infer, or decide now. |
| 作者标注残留: 章末的、本卷完、第二卷开始 | Delete and replace with in-world image, action, or transition. |
| 面目模糊词: 黑影、某种东西 | Use concrete physical identity, visible feature, or role. |
| 精确判断过武断 | Turn into inference unless canon/source proves it. |
| 医学/科学/系统术语泄漏 | Use character-level perception unless the POV has reason to use the term. |
| 感官堆叠过密 | Keep one main sensation per paragraph; move or delete the rest. |
| 常识词汇低级错字 | Check idioms, body terms, and fixed expressions. |
| 过度规整段落 | Break paragraph symmetry; follow attention, interruption, and pressure. |
| 大纲字段顺序外露 | Hide structure; rewrite by POV attention chain. |
| 每段都解释意义 | Let action, object, silence, or dialogue carry meaning. |
| Humanizer-zh 通用AI词 / 空泛强调: 此外、至关重要、深入、彰显、格局、赋予、承载、无缝、直观、充满活力 | Delete or replace only when used as empty emphasis. Preserve strong wording when it serves genre intensity, character voice, pressure, humor, suspense,爽点, rhythm, or emotional payoff. |
| 金句式总结 | Keep only deliberate hooks or character voice; otherwise ground it in a visible choice or consequence. |
| 通用积极结论 | Replace with a concrete unresolved pressure, aftertaste, or next action. |
| 三段式列举 | Break the neat rhythm; choose the two strongest details or an uneven sequence driven by attention. |
| 过度限定 | Keep uncertainty only when it reflects POV knowledge limits; otherwise write the direct perception. |

| 悬空动作 / 假细节 | Give the action a function: pressure, concealment, preparation, hesitation, exposure, consequence, or another character's reaction. Delete it if it only decorates mood. |
| 连续短句断链 | Short sentences may break rhythm, but the action chain must still show cause, pressure, or next choice. |

## Scene Presence

To make a scene feel lived-in:

- Anchor location through one concrete object or pressure source.
- Give the body a state: pain, cold, hunger, breath, fatigue, restraint, heat, numbness, or stillness.
- Let objects respond: chair scrapes, phone lights, cloth sticks, cup tilts, door shudders.
- Let environment push back: wind enters, light flickers, smell catches, floor slips, crowd turns.
- Let tactical thought stay short and tied to the next action.
- Check small gestures: a hand stopping, eyes moving, breath catching, fingers tightening, or a step pausing must either reveal a state, hide an impulse, prepare an action, trigger a response, or change the next beat.

## Self-Check

Before delivering or finalizing a narrative unit, check:

- Is the first paragraph easy to enter?
- Does the unit open with pressure, motion, voice, image, or problem?
- Does every long sentence have a reason to stay long?
- Is every paragraph focused on one main movement or feeling?
- Does the prose avoid westernized syntax (translation-ese), unnecessary passive voice, passive sensory mediation, and redundant logic connectors, while preserving natural 被字句 that carry pressure, victimhood, restraint, hidden actor, idiom, or character voice?
- Does dialogue sound like these people in this relationship?
- Does each major dialogue exchange move story, conflict, or relationship?
- Are abstract emotions shown through action or texture first?
- Are there hard transitions, slogan-like summaries, or explanation-heavy bridges?
- Does the text trust the reader, or does it keep proving and explaining what the scene already shows?
- Are there empty Humanizer-zh AI residues: generic emphasis, three-part lists, quote-like maxims, vague "importance" claims, or cheerful generic conclusions? Did the edit preserve genre-appropriate rhetorical intensity instead of flattening strong but functional wording?
- Are key lines and hooks visually clear on mobile?
- Does the prose avoid visible outline-field order?
- Do paragraph lengths vary by pressure rather than template?
- Do small gestures have a clear story function instead of floating as decorative restraint?
- Can each short standalone sentence connect to pressure, attention, consequence, or choice?
- Did something change by the end?
